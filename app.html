Heatsink Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page works out junction temperatures, heatsink budgets and dissipation limits from the numbers you type: the power times the thermal resistance for the rise, a temperature budget minus the fixed drops for the heatsink, and the budget over the total resistance for the limit. The temperatures, resistances and power are yours to measure or read off your own datasheet. It treats each thermal resistance as fixed, so it has not seen your build and does not know the mounting, the airflow, the interface material or the part variant, and it gives no verdict that a cooling setup is adequate or safe. Every number here is your own entry.

The junction truth

Where the junction lands: the power times the total resistance, on top of ambient.

The heatsink truth

The most heatsink the budget can carry: the rise to spend, minus the fixed drops, turned back into °C/W.

The headroom truth

The most the setup can dissipate: the rise to spend over the total resistance.
